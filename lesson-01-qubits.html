Quantum mechanics & qubits.

A qubit is the basic unit of quantum information. It behaves differently from the bits inside an ordinary computer.

Classical bits vs. qubits

A classical bit must be 0 or 1. A qubit can be in a combination of 0 and 1 before it is measured. This combination is called a quantum state.

Dirac notation

We write the basic states as |0⟩ and |1⟩. A general qubit is written as a|0⟩ + b|1⟩, where the coefficients describe measurement probabilities.

Measurement collapse

Measurement gives one definite result: 0 or 1. The superposition disappears after measurement, and the result is guided by the probabilities in the state.
