Lesson 02 / Single qubits

Gates and superposition.

Quantum gates rotate or change a qubit without destroying its state.

The essential gates

Pauli-X acts like a bit flip. Pauli-Y and Z rotate the state in different directions. The S and T gates change phase, which affects interference later.

Hadamard gate

The Hadamard gate, written H, turns |0⟩ into an even superposition of 0 and 1. It is often the first step in a quantum algorithm.

Bloch Sphere

The Bloch Sphere is a visual map of one qubit. The north and south poles represent 0 and 1, while points between them represent superpositions.

Try it mentally

Apply H to a qubit in state 0. What two outcomes should you expect when it is measured?
