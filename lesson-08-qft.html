Lesson 08 / Fourier tools

Phase estimation & QFT.

The Quantum Fourier Transform helps expose hidden periodic patterns in quantum data.

Fourier intuition

A Fourier transform changes how we describe a signal: instead of positions, we focus on frequencies. The QFT does a similar change for amplitudes.

Phase estimation

QPE estimates the phase associated with an eigenvalue of an operation. Controlled operations write phase information into an extra register.

Period finding

Repeating patterns can reveal the period of a function. That period becomes useful information for factoring and other quantum algorithms.
