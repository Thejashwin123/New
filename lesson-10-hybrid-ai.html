Lesson 10 / Hybrid computing

Variational quantum & AI.

Hybrid algorithms combine a small quantum circuit with a classical optimizer.

The variational loop

A parameterized circuit produces a result. A classical computer scores that result and adjusts the parameters. The loop repeats until the score improves.

VQE and QAOA

VQE estimates the lowest energy of a molecule. QAOA explores hard optimization problems such as choosing the best route or schedule.

Why NISQ matters

Current devices have limited, noisy qubits. Hybrid methods keep quantum circuits small and let classical computers handle the heavier coordination.
