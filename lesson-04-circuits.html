Lesson 04 / Circuit design

Quantum circuits & logic.

A quantum circuit is a visual recipe for preparing, changing, and measuring qubits.

Reading a circuit

Horizontal wires represent qubits. Gates are placed from left to right, showing the order in which operations happen.

Registers and measurement

A quantum register is a group of qubits treated as one system. Measurement converts the quantum state into classical information that a computer can read.

Reversible logic

Quantum gates are reversible: each operation has an inverse. This protects information during the computation and is different from many ordinary logic operations.
