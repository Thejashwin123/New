Lesson 09 / Factoring

Shor's algorithm.

Shor's algorithm uses quantum period finding to factor large composite numbers.

The classical problem

Multiplying two large primes is easy, but finding those primes from their product is difficult for classical computers. RSA relies on this asymmetry.

Quantum order-finding

The quantum part uses QPE and the QFT to find the period of a modular function. That period can reveal factors through a classical calculation.

Hybrid workflow

A practical run alternates quantum sampling with classical post-processing. Large fault-tolerant machines would be needed to threaten real RSA keys.
