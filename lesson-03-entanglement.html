Lesson 03 / Multi-qubit systems

Meet entanglement.

Entanglement links the states of qubits so that they must be described together.

Combining qubits

A system of two qubits has four basic states: 00, 01, 10, and 11. Tensor products let us describe all of those possibilities in one state.

CNOT gate

CNOT flips a target qubit only when its control qubit is 1. Combined with H, it can create a Bell State, a simple example of entanglement.

What measurement means

Measuring one entangled qubit gives information about the other. This does not send a usable message faster than light; the correlation was created when the qubits interacted.
