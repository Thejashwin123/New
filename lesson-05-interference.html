Lesson 05 / Quantum behavior

Phase and interference.

Quantum algorithms use wave-like behavior to strengthen useful answers and cancel unhelpful ones.

Phase is not probability

Global phase cannot be observed by itself. Relative phase between parts of a state matters because it changes how those parts interfere.

Constructive and destructive

In constructive interference, amplitudes add together. In destructive interference, they cancel. The final measurement probabilities reflect that combination.

Phase kickback

In a controlled operation, a phase change can appear on the control qubit even though the operation acts on the target. This effect is central to many algorithms.
